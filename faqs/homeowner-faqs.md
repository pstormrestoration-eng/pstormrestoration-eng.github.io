# Homeowner FAQs

Answers drawn from phoenixstormgroup.com. See [Phoenix Storm Restoration](../business/phoenix-storm-restoration.md).

**Do I need a new roof or just a repair?**
An inspection looks at the roof's age, condition and any hail or wind damage, then Phoenix recommends one or the other. See [Roof inspection](../services/roof-inspection.md).

**Are inspections and estimates free?**
Yes, with photo documentation.

**How long does a roof replacement take?**
Most homes take one day. Specialty roofs take longer; see [Specialty roofing](../services/specialty-roofing.md).

**Are you licensed and insured?**
Texas doesn't license roofers. Phoenix is fully insured, an NTRCA member, and holds HAAG and IKO certifications.

**What's the warranty?**
A 5-year workmanship warranty from installation day.

**Should I file a claim?**
Phoenix helps decide after the inspection, based on what it finds. See [Insurance claims assistance](../business/insurance-claims.md).

**Do I have to pay my deductible?**
Yes. Phoenix will not waive or absorb it.

**Do you come out during a storm?**
No. Call during the storm to get on the list; crews tarp and inspect once it passes. See [24/7 emergency response](../services/24-7-emergency-response.md).

**Do you handle interior water damage?**
No. Phoenix doesn't do interior flood restoration or fire or water mitigation.

**Do I need to be home?**
Not for tarping, inspection or installation, though you should be reachable by phone.

**What if it rains on install day?**
The job is rescheduled if rain is likely within 24 hours.

**When do I pay?**
Retail jobs: a deposit at signing and the balance at completion. Insurance jobs are paid in stages as the insurer releases funds. See [How Phoenix runs a job](../business/how-phoenix-runs-a-job.md).

**Do you offer financing?**
Yes, through outside lenders, including six-months-interest-free options for qualifying customers. See [Financing](../business/financing.md).

**How do I schedule an inspection?**
Call or text (945) 308-0425 or use https://www.phoenixstormgroup.com/contact.
