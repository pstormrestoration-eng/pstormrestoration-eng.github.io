# Certifications and partners

Credentials shown by [Phoenix Storm Restoration](phoenix-storm-restoration.md):
- **HAAG Certified** residential roof inspectors
- **IKO Craftsman Premier** certified contractor
- **BBB Accredited**
- **NTRCA**: member of the North Texas Roofing Contractors Association

## Licensing and insurance
Texas does not issue a state roofing license. Phoenix says it is fully insured and keeps its own certifications for quality assurance.

## Manufacturers and suppliers
Partner logos on the site: ABC Supply, Atlas, Beacon, Brava, CertainTeed, GAF, IKO, Owens Corning, SRS, TAMKO and Tarco. Featured products include IKO Dynasty and IKO Nordic (Class 4 impact rated) shingles and Sheffield Metal standing seam.

## Warranty
A 5-year workmanship warranty that starts on the day the roof is installed. Manufacturer warranties apply to the materials.
