# Insurance claims assistance

[Phoenix Storm Restoration](phoenix-storm-restoration.md) helps homeowners through hail and wind damage claims. It documents damage, attends the adjuster's inspection, builds estimates in Xactimate and has an in-house supplement team.

## Steps
1. **Inspection:** a free, HAAG-standard inspection.
2. **Call the insurer:** if there's damage, Phoenix helps the homeowner file, and the carrier schedules an adjuster.
3. **Adjuster appointment:** the Phoenix team attends, shares its findings and points out other storm damage such as gutters, windows, siding and fencing.
4. **Scope review:** the carrier's scope is checked line by line, and supplements are requested for missed items.
5. **If approved:** project planning, the agreement, and a pre-installation attic check for gas and A/C lines.
6. **If denied:** review the decision, request a reinspection, look at retail options, or keep the file open for a future storm.

Supplement work is for homeowners under contract with Phoenix.

## Deductibles
The homeowner pays the deductible. Phoenix will not waive or absorb it, citing Texas law.

## Premiums
Phoenix's FAQ says storm claims are generally treated as acts of nature and usually don't raise an individual's premium, though carriers may raise rates for a whole area. Homeowners should confirm with their own insurer.
