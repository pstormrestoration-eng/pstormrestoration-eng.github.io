# Phoenix Storm Restoration

Phoenix Storm Restoration is a locally owned, family-run residential roofing and storm restoration company in Grapevine, Texas. Its website is phoenixstormgroup.com. The company describes itself as faith-based and family-oriented, with more than 10 years of experience. Its CEO and owner is [Dustin K.](leadership.md).

The founders say they started the company after hiring the wrong roofer for their own home, and they built it around better service, homeowner education and trust.

## What Phoenix does
- [24/7 emergency response](../services/24-7-emergency-response.md): tarping, leak mitigation and emergency repairs after hail and wind
- [Roof inspections](../services/roof-inspection.md) by HAAG-certified inspectors, free, with a photo report
- [Roof replacement](../services/roof-installation.md), [specialty roofing](../services/specialty-roofing.md) and [solar detach and reinstall](../services/solar-detach-reinstall.md)
- [Insurance claims assistance](insurance-claims.md), including adjuster meetings and supplements
- [Outdoor lighting](phoenix-lighting.md) through its Phoenix Lighting division

The full list is in the [services index](../services/index.md).

## Who Phoenix works for
Homeowners. The site is built around residential roofing, especially roofs damaged by hail and wind.

## Where Phoenix works
Grapevine, Tarrant County and Dallas County, and the wider Dallas–Fort Worth area. See [Service area](../service-areas/service-area.md).

## Key facts
- Office in Grapevine, Texas; emergency line answered 24/7 (see [Contact and hours](contact-and-hours.md))
- HAAG-certified inspectors, IKO Craftsman Premier certified, BBB accredited, NTRCA member (see [Certifications and partners](certifications-and-partners.md))
- 5-year workmanship warranty, starting on installation day
- Most residential roof replacements are finished in one day, depending on size, materials and weather
- Emergency arrival target of 60 to 90 minutes in the Grapevine area when conditions allow
- Will not waive or absorb an insurance deductible

## Mission and values
The stated mission is to build leaders, people and community, not just roofs. The vision is to be the most trusted roofing and storm restoration company in North Texas. Its values spell WERISE: Winning mentality, Excellence, Resilience, Integrity, Service, Execution.
