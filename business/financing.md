# Financing

[Phoenix Storm Restoration](phoenix-storm-restoration.md) offers financing through outside lenders: Contractor Loan Pro, GreenSky and HFS Financial. Loans are originated by the participating financial institutions, not by Phoenix.

- Homeowners can pre-qualify online. Decisions usually come within 24 hours.
- Some programs offer six months interest-free for qualifying customers.
- Financing can cover a retail roof or the homeowner's deductible on an insurance job, subject to approval.

The site doesn't publish rates or terms. Those come from the lender.
