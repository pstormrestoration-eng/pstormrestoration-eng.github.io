# Emergency Roof Inspection

Service by [Phoenix Storm Restoration](../business/phoenix-storm-restoration.md). Live page: https://www.phoenixstormgroup.com/emergency-response/roof-inspection

A storm damage inspection done soon after severe weather, ideally within 72 hours, before more weather wipes out evidence or causes secondary damage. Part of [24/7 emergency response](24-7-emergency-response.md).

## Three levels
- **Ground:** downspouts, garage doors, siding, trim, fencing, HVAC units, windows, screens and shutters
- **Roof:** every slope, penetrations, vents and pipe jacks, chimneys, flashing, skylights, gutters, and structures such as pergolas or solar panels
- **Attic:** leaks, water intrusion, decking and ventilation

The homeowner gets a written report with photos, measurements and repair recommendations to support the claim. Phoenix can tarp the roof on the spot and coordinate with the adjuster.
