# Roof Replacement and Installation

Service by [Phoenix Storm Restoration](../business/phoenix-storm-restoration.md). Live page: https://www.phoenixstormgroup.com/roofing-services/roof-installation

Residential roof replacement in Grapevine, Tarrant County and Dallas County. Most homes are done in one day, depending on size, materials and weather.

## Materials
- Architectural and impact-resistant shingles for hail and wind
- Designer shingles
- Specialty and metal roofing (see [Specialty roofing](specialty-roofing.md))
- Low-slope systems (TPO, modified bitumen) for porches, additions and light commercial areas

## Installation steps
1. **Protection and tear-off:** landscaping, siding and outdoor spaces are covered with tarps and plywood, then the old roof comes off.
2. **Deck check:** rotted or weak decking is replaced.
3. **Underlayment and flashing:** waterproof underlayment, drip edge and flashing, with extra attention to valleys, chimneys and skylights.
4. **Roof system:** materials go on to manufacturer specifications, with ventilation and accessories.
5. **Quality control and cleanup:** a project manager does a final walkthrough, and a magnetic sweep picks up nails.

Homeowners get a Certificate of Completion. Impact-resistant shingle documentation can help with insurance records and possible premium discounts. Phoenix backs the work with a 5-year workmanship warranty.
