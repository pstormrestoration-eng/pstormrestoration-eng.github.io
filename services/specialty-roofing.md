# Specialty Roofing

Service by [Phoenix Storm Restoration](../business/phoenix-storm-restoration.md). Live page: https://www.phoenixstormgroup.com/roofing-services/specialty-roofing

Roofs that aren't standard asphalt shingle.

## Materials
- **Tile:** clay and concrete, including Spanish, Mission and slate-look styles. Phoenix pays close attention to the underlayment, flashing and fasteners, since that's usually what hail and wind damage on a tile roof.
- **Stone-coated steel:** lighter than tile, with good hail and wind resistance, in tile, shake or slate profiles
- **Metal:** standing seam for clean lines, long life and wind and hail performance, with impact-rated options
- **Slate** and **synthetic** roofs
- **Designer asphalt shingles** that mimic slate or wood shake

## Typical install times
Designer asphalt 1 to 3 days, metal 3 to 5 days, synthetic 4 to 7 days, tile 1 to 2 weeks, slate 1 to 2 weeks or more. These are estimates.

## Lifespan
Slate and tile 50 to 100 years; metal 40 to 70 years.

## Insurance
When covered storm damage hits a specialty roof, the policy usually pays to replace it with the same kind of material.
