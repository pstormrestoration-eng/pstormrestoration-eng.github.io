# Emergency Roof Tarping

Service by [Phoenix Storm Restoration](../business/phoenix-storm-restoration.md). Live page: https://www.phoenixstormgroup.com/emergency-response/roof-tarping

Temporary tarping that stops water from getting into the house after storm damage, especially hail. It's a stopgap until the roof is repaired or replaced. Part of [24/7 emergency response](24-7-emergency-response.md).

- A safety check of the damaged area comes first.
- Commercial-grade tarps stocked at the Grapevine facility, fastened for wind resistance and drainage.
- Response is usually the same day, with a 60 to 90 minute arrival target in the Grapevine area when conditions allow.
- Tarps last several weeks while the claim and permanent repairs move forward.
- Most policies require homeowners to take prompt steps to limit further damage. Tarping does that, and Phoenix documents it for the claim.
