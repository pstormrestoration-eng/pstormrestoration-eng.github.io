# Roof Inspection

Service by [Phoenix Storm Restoration](../business/phoenix-storm-restoration.md). Live page: https://www.phoenixstormgroup.com/roofing-services/roof-inspection

Storm and maintenance inspections by HAAG-certified inspectors. Inspections and estimates are free, and most take 10 to 20 minutes.

## What's checked
- **Roof surface:** shingle condition, seal, granule loss, uplift and creases
- **Details:** flashing, valleys, pipe boots, storm collars, skylights and chimneys
- **Water paths:** eaves, gutters, downspouts, wall transitions and penetrations
- **Storm signs:** hail strikes, wind-driven rain entry and debris impacts

The check covers the ground level, every roof slope, and the attic when the roof shows damage.

## What the homeowner gets
- A photo report of the roof, gutters, windows and exterior, usable for records or an insurance carrier
- A storm recovery guide with space to track insurance communication
- Recommendations sorted into urgent repairs and items to watch
- Clear next steps: file a claim, schedule repairs or plan maintenance

If there's no damage, Phoenix says so. It recommends an inspection every six months and after major storms.
