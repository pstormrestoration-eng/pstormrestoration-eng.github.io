# 24/7 Emergency Response

Service by [Phoenix Storm Restoration](../business/phoenix-storm-restoration.md). Live page: https://www.phoenixstormgroup.com/emergency-response/24-7-response

Phoenix answers storm calls 24/7 and sends crews for tarping, board-up, leak mitigation and emergency repairs after hail and wind.

## How it works
1. **Dispatch:** the call goes to the Grapevine dispatch center, which sends the nearest certified technician.
2. **Safety check:** the tech looks for structural, electrical and debris hazards, secures the area and gives the household safety guidance.
3. **Mitigation:** emergency tarping and temporary weatherproofing to stop further damage.

Afterward the homeowner gets photos and damage documentation, then a full inspection and a talk about next steps.

## Timing
- Arrival target of 60 to 90 minutes in the Grapevine area when conditions allow.
- Crews do not climb roofs during an active storm. Call during the storm to get on the schedule; they come once it passes.
- A tarp holds for several weeks. Permanent repair or replacement should follow.

## Not covered
Interior flood restoration and fire or water mitigation.
