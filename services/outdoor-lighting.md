# Outdoor Lighting

Service by [Phoenix Storm Restoration](../business/phoenix-storm-restoration.md). Live page: https://www.phoenixstormgroup.com/outdoor-lighting

Outdoor lighting from [Phoenix Lighting](../business/phoenix-lighting.md), the company's lighting division.

## Permanent roofline lighting
Haven X Series track lighting mounted along the eaves, color-matched to the trim so it's hard to see by day. Options include Evolution, Classic and Genesis; Genesis is pitched for strict HOA communities. The lights can run warm white every night or holiday and team colors on a schedule, all from an app.

## Landscape lighting
- Haven 9 Series for trees, columns and facades, in color; 9 Series Pro adds motion
- Haven 6 Series white light, adjustable from cool to warm
- Haven Q Series for patios, steps and retaining walls

Uplighting, path and step lighting, pools and water features, and smart controls are also listed.

## Christmas light installation
Commercial-grade LED lights clipped to the roofline, with design, in-season maintenance and takedown after New Year's.

## What's included
A free custom design and on-site estimate; installation and testing; app setup, with the crew showing the homeowner how to use it. Price depends on the roofline's linear footage and complexity. Permanent lighting can go in any time of year.
