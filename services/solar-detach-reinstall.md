# Solar Panel Detach and Reinstall

Service by [Phoenix Storm Restoration](../business/phoenix-storm-restoration.md). Live page: https://www.phoenixstormgroup.com/roofing-services/solar-detach-reinstall

Taking solar panels off so the roof underneath can be replaced, then putting them back. Roofing over panels risks leaks, bad flashing and damage to modules, wiring and racking.

## How it works
1. Once the roof job is scheduled, Phoenix sends the details to its solar partner.
2. The partner inspects the system, writes an estimate and sends it to the insurance company.
3. After approval, the dates are set to match the roof schedule.
4. The partner's licensed, insured crew removes the panels before roofing and reinstalls them afterward.

It usually takes 1 to 3 days. Panels typically come off the day before or the morning of the reroof. The house stays on the grid the whole time, and the system is tested before it's turned back on. Phoenix handles permits, inspections and utility sign-offs where they're required.

## Insurance
On hail or wind claims, detach and reinstall can be part of the claim scope. The solar partner submits its estimate straight to the insurer.
