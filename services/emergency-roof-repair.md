# Emergency Roof Repair

Service by [Phoenix Storm Restoration](../business/phoenix-storm-restoration.md). Live page: https://www.phoenixstormgroup.com/emergency-response/roof-repair

Emergency repairs after storms, hail and high wind in Grapevine and across DFW. Part of [24/7 emergency response](24-7-emergency-response.md).

## Typical repairs
- **Leaks:** sealing punctures or cracks from hail or wind
- **Missing shingles:** replacing them, or covering the area so the roof sheds water
- **Exposed or damaged skylights:** securing the opening with emergency tarping

Repairs include temporary patching and membranes where needed. Damage is documented with photos, measurements and material notes for the insurance claim.
